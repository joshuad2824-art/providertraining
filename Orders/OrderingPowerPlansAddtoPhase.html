Overview:  A PowerPlan is a care planning tool that allows providers the ability to manage orders, outcomes, and interventions as they relate to an individualized patient-specific, problem-driven plan of care. Like care sets, PowerPlans are also order sets, but have many additional benefits, such as:

  • •Proactive planning of orders prior to activation.
  • •Proactive duplicate checking.
  • •Automatic or manual plan update options.
  • •Adding additional orders on the fly.
  • •Ability to discontinue the whole plan without having to discontinue each order component individually.

NOTE:  PowerPlan icon, often remembered as the “pizza box” icon,is displayed to the left of the plan name in the search results, making it very easy to recognize plans versus care sets, and individual orders. PowerPlans combine a group of orders into a single order to expedite the process.  

Ordering a PowerPlan

IMPORTANT! Many times you can order using the patient's problem or diagnosis to generate the available PowerPlans. You can also utilize PowerPlans when placing many Admission and Discharge Orders.

  1. To add a PowerPlan to a patient's medical record, you will search for the specific plan in the Orders window just as you would an individual orderable.
  2. Click on the  Icon in the Orders screen.  In the Orders Profile screen Search for your PowerPlan.

  1. Select the appropriate order and click Done.

IMPORTANT! Notice some of the components are already preselected for items almost always ordered. You have the option of keeping or deselecting most of the pre-selected orders within the plan

  1. Complete the order details by selecting  or deselecting the pre-built order options within the order!

IMPORTANT! Pre-selected items that are shaded in are mandatory and must be ordered as part of the chosen PowerPlan

  1. Once you complete the order, select  Plan for Later or Initiate Now.

IMPORTANT! If you select Plan for Later, the RN will initiate the orders at the appropriate time.  You will select Initiate Now, if you want to IMMEDIATELY act on the orders. 

PowerPlan SubPhase Orders

  1. There sometime will be Subphase Orders within the PowerPlan.  These orders require you to complete additional details.  The Subphase Order icon resembles a double “pizza box”.
  2. Click on the Order Name, next to the subphase icon, to open the order detail screen.

.

  1. The Orders Detail screen will open.  Select the order options to be included in the subphase order.  
  2. Click on the “Return to….” tab at the bottom or top of the order screen to return to the main order.

  1. Once you are on the main Orders Detail page, complete the order by Initiating it or Planning it for later as outlined in step #5.

Add to Phase

All PowerPlans hold the most common orders used by physicians, based on a patient's Problem and/or Diagnosis, however there may be individual orders that you would like to have as part of a PowerPlan.  You can add individual orders to the phase of the PowerPlan, by completing the following steps. Suggestions for Wording

  1. Select the blue plus sign near Add to Phase in the Order Screen toolbar.

  1. Select the drop down arrow and choose the option you would like to order.  

NOTE: You are able to order an Outcome/Intervention, A Medication Order or choose to Add a Prescription to be sent to the patient’s pharmacy.

  1. Begin typing the name of the  order you want to add to the PowerPlan and choose your option.

  1. Select Done.
  2. Click Orders for Signature at the bottom right hand of the screen.
  3. Click Sign   at the bottom right of the screen.
  4. You will come to the main Orders page and you will see the words “Processing Please Refresh”.
  5. Refresh your screen by clicking on the chasing arrows at the top right in  the Orders screen.

  1. Once you Refresh you will see the PowerPlan with the added Order listed under the corresponding heading.

NOTE:  Any order Added to Phase will populate under it’s corresponding heading within the PowerPlan, i.e. vital signs, referrals, nursing orders, IV’s, etc.

  1. Complete the order by clicking on Sign at the bottom right of the screen.

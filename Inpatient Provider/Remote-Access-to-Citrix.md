# Remote Access to Citrix / MVD Applications

**Audience:** General use
**Date:** Mar 13, 2026

## Overview
How to reach MVD applications when you're off the Ascension St. John network — from a personal or designated work computer.

## Steps
1. Go to https://cloud.ascension.org/
   - On site, you can still use MVD from the desktop Citrix Portal icon.
2. Sign in to the remote Citrix portal with your `domain\network-username` and password. Example: `SJMC\UName01`.
3. Under **Apps**, you'll see the icons you have access to.
   - Missing applications? Ask your manager or the medical staff office to have MVD applications added.
4. To pin favorites to your **Home** tab, find the app icon and click the **star**.
5. Starred favorites appear on the **Home** page.

## Published Desktop (full internet capabilities)
Open a full desktop to use all internal network links.

> **NOTE:** The Clinical Imaging Viewer launched from Cerner is only available on the Ascension network. The normal workflow above tries to open it on your non-Ascension network and fails — use the Published Desktop instead.

1. Select **Desktops**, then click the available **OKTUL Published Desktop**.
2. You can now open Cerner PowerChart and view images from radiology reports.

## Troubleshooting
1. Make sure you have the latest Citrix receiver installed.
2. Use the latest stable Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
3. Missing MVD applications must be requested by your manager.

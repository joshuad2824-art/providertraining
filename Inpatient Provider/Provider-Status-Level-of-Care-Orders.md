# Provider Status / Level of Care Orders (PSO)

**Audience:** Physicians
**System:** Cerner PowerChart

## Overview
Provider Status Change / Level of Care Orders (PSO) support **order-driven billing** based on the patient's status and level of care.

> **IMPORTANT:** "Level of Care" defines what the hospital can bill. The goal is to reduce denials through clear clinical definition and accuracy of patient status and level of care.

## Available status / level-of-care orders
There are several status-change options under **Patient Status Orders**, including **Patient Status Change Orders**, **Admit Status Orders (ASO)**, and **Discharge Status Orders (DSO)**.

A Patient Status Order (PSO):
- Simplifies ordering based on patient scenarios
- Simplifies order entry and prevents inadvertent changes to status, expected length of hospital care, and level of care
- Helps nursing communicate transfer needs within the provider's ordered level of care
- Sends notifications to Registration/Admissions and the Room Control / Patient Logistics Center (PLC) for patient movement

> **NOTE:** Place Patient Status Orders **in addition to** the appropriate PowerPlans for the inpatient stay.

## Steps
1. Go to the **Orders** component to view the Order Profile (orders are grouped by Activity Type). Review current orders before adding.
2. Select the appropriate **Patient Status Change**, **Admit (ASO)**, or **Discharge (DSO)** order from the main menu.
3. Select the order and complete the order details.
4. Click **Done**.

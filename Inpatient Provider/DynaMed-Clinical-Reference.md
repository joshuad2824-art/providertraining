# DynaMed Clinical Reference — Getting Started

**Audience:** All Clinicians
**Date:** Mar 25, 2022

## Overview
DynaMed is an evidence-based reference subscription available to all Ascension St. John associates and affiliated medical staff. This guide covers creating a personal EBSCO DynaMed account, connecting to the Ascension subscription, turning on CME tracking, and setting up the mobile app.

> **IMPORTANT:** You must be at an Ascension St. John location and on the network to link to the Ascension subscription. After creating an account and signing in, you can access DynaMed from anywhere.

## Steps
1. Go to www.dynamed.com.
2. Click the **person icon** (upper right).
3. Click **Register now** and complete the form.
   - **NOTE:** You must consent to personal data collection to use DynaMed off the network and to track CME/MOC.
4. Click the **CME ticker** (upper right, next to the person icon).
5. Click the **Settings** icon and adjust settings to receive the appropriate CME or MOC credits.
6. On your mobile device, connect to the **AH-AIA** Wi-Fi network.
7. In the iOS App Store or Google Play, search **DynaMed**.
8. Download and open the app.
9. Tap **Institutional Network** → **Authenticate** → choose a download-content method.

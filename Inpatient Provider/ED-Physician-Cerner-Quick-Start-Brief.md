## How to use this kit

Three things work together:

- **The job aids** — official Ascension St. John step-by-steps, one per task.
- **The index** (`00_Job-Aids-Index.md`) — the table of contents; scan it to find the right aid.
- **Your ChatGPT project** — ask a plain-English question and it answers from the job aids first.

**Source-of-truth order, highest to lowest:** a live answer from Clinical Informatics → the job aid → ChatGPT's general knowledge. If ChatGPT and a job aid ever disagree, the job aid wins. If a job aid and our team ever disagree, call us.

## Your environment in 30 seconds

You're on **Cerner PowerChart** with **FirstNet** as the ED interface — build **2018.01**. That version detail matters for one reason: most Cerner / Oracle Health screenshots and tips you'll find online describe newer versions, so the menus and labels there may not match your screen. The aids in this kit are written for what's actually live here — when in doubt, trust the aid over the internet.

- **Dictation** is **Dragon Medical One (DMO)** — voice-to-text into your notes.
- **Controlled-substance prescribing** uses **Imprivata** two-factor sign-off (EPCS).
- **Off-campus / personal device** — reach Cerner through **AH-AIA** Wi-Fi on site, or the **cloud.ascension.org** remote portal off site.

## Ground rules for the ChatGPT assistant

It's scoped to **software and documentation help only**. It will:

- answer how-to questions on Cerner / FirstNet and the systems above;
- **not** give clinical, diagnostic, or treatment advice — that's not its job;
- **never** ask for or accept patient information (PHI); keep real patient data out of it and use made-up examples;
- send you back to Clinical Informatics when a question is out of scope or it isn't sure.

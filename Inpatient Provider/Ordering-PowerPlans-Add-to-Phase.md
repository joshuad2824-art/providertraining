# Ordering PowerPlans / Add to Phase

**Audience:** Providers
**System:** Cerner PowerChart

## Overview
A **PowerPlan** is a care-planning tool for managing orders, outcomes, and interventions as part of an individualized, problem-driven plan of care. Like care sets, PowerPlans are order sets — but with extra benefits:
- Proactive planning of orders before activation
- Proactive duplicate checking
- Automatic or manual plan updates
- Adding orders on the fly
- Discontinuing the whole plan at once (no need to stop each order individually)

> **NOTE:** The PowerPlan icon (the "pizza box") sits to the left of the plan name in search results, making plans easy to tell apart from care sets and individual orders.

## Ordering a PowerPlan
> **TIP:** You can often generate available PowerPlans from the patient's problem or diagnosis. PowerPlans are also useful for admission and discharge orders.

1. In the **Orders** window, search for the plan as you would any orderable.
2. Click the icon in the Orders screen; in the Orders Profile, search for your PowerPlan.
3. Select the plan and click **Done**.
   - **IMPORTANT:** Some components come pre-selected for near-universal items. You can keep or deselect most pre-selected orders.
4. Complete order details by selecting/deselecting the pre-built options.
   - **IMPORTANT:** Pre-selected items that are **shaded** are mandatory and must be ordered as part of the plan.
5. Choose **Plan for Later** or **Initiate Now**.
   - **Plan for Later** = the RN initiates the orders at the appropriate time. **Initiate Now** = act on the orders immediately.

## PowerPlan subphase orders
6. Some PowerPlans contain **subphase orders** needing extra detail. The subphase icon looks like a double "pizza box."
7. Click the order name next to the subphase icon to open the detail screen.
8. Select the options to include in the subphase order.
9. Click the **"Return to…"** tab (top or bottom) to go back to the main order.
10. On the main Orders Detail page, complete by **Initiate Now** or **Plan for Later** (per step 5).

## Add to Phase (adding individual orders to a plan)
PowerPlans hold the most common orders for a problem/diagnosis, but you can add individual orders to a phase:

11. Click the blue **+** near **Add to Phase** in the Orders toolbar.
12. Use the dropdown to choose the order type — an Outcome/Intervention, a medication order, or a prescription to send to the patient's pharmacy.
13. Start typing the order name and select it.
14. Click **Done**.
15. Click **Orders for Signature** (bottom-right).
16. Click **Sign**.
17. The main Orders page shows "Processing — Please Refresh."
18. Refresh via the chasing-arrows icon (top-right of the Orders screen).
19. After refresh, the added order appears under its corresponding heading in the PowerPlan (vital signs, referrals, nursing orders, IVs, etc.).
20. Complete by clicking **Sign** (bottom-right).

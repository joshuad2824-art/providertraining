# Getting Started with Cerner Mobile Apps

**Audience:** Medical Staff & OB Nurses
**Apps:** PowerChart Touch, Cerner Message Center, FetaLink+

## Overview
Ascension St. John lets clinicians access Cerner features from personal smartphones.

- **PowerChart Touch** — an EHR extension to find your patient list/schedule, review the chart, create notes with templates and dictation, edit notes, and place simple basic orders.
- **Cerner Message Center** — review and send messages and sign orders on the go.
- **FetaLink+** — for OB physicians, residents, APRNs, PAs, and hospital maternal/child nurses only.

## Getting started
> Complete these steps on each device you plan to use.

1. Search **Cerner** in the App Store and download one or more of:
   - PowerChart Touch
   - Cerner Message Center
   - FetaLink+ *(Apple iOS only; Android pending FDA approval)*
2. Launch the app. The first app will prompt for your **access code**.
3. After entering the code, tap **Go** (bottom-right of the keypad) — do **not** select the field just below "Enter Access Code." (You'll get an error if the phone has no cellular or Wi-Fi signal.)
4. Sign in with your existing Cerner username and password.

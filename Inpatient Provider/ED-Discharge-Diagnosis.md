# ED Note Remodel — Discharge Diagnosis

**Audience:** Emergency Room Providers
**System:** Cerner FirstNet
**Go-live:** current

## Overview
Shows ED providers how to document **ICD-10 diagnosis codes** in the new note system and fold it into their existing workflow.

## Discharge diagnosis — step by step
1. Before completing your note, go to your **ED Provider** workflow or your **Discharge/Admit** workflow.
2. Navigate to the **Problem list** (ED Provider workflow) or **Discharge Diagnosis** (Discharge/Admit workflow).
3. Go to **Add Problem**.
4. Start typing the diagnosis and select the appropriate one.
5. **Chronic problems**
   - **REQUIREMENT:** The discharge diagnosis component requires a chronic problem to be completed.
   - There is an option to select **No chronic problems**.
   - To enter a chronic discharge problem, toggle the search from **This visit** to **Chronic** (or **This visit and chronic**), then search.
6. To use the familiar problem list:
   - Select the header hyperlink to jump to **Problems and Diagnoses** in the chart's table of contents.
   - Enter the diagnosis as before.
   - Select **Back** to return to your workflow.
7. **Assessment and Plan selection** (in the clinical workspace) — use the dropdown to choose:
   - **Outpatient – Assessment and Plan** (includes orders from the ED visit), or
   - **Diagnosis only – Assessment and Plan** (diagnosis only).
8. Complete the rest of the discharge (see **ED Note Remodel — Discharge Workflow** for details).

## Trauma global phrases — procedures
To use the Trauma Procedures, type `<shift>` in the free-text field under Procedure.

## Restraints & procedure consent
> Created to satisfy **TJC** (The Joint Commission) requirements for restraints and procedural consent. Available in PowerNote ED and Dynamic Documentation (inpatient provider notes).
- Type `\Restraints` to place the restraints phrase in a note.
- Type `\Consent` to place the consent phrase in a note.

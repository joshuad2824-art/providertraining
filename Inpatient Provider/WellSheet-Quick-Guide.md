# WellSheet Quick Guide

**Audience:** Providers / Nursing / Case Management
**System:** Cerner (WellSheet overlay)
**Date:** 3/21/2025

## Overview
WellSheet is a rounding/handoff tool that integrates with Cerner, letting you view your patient list and each patient's pertinent details (labs, imaging results, vitals, etc.) in one view.

- **Where to find it:** the top toolbar of Cerner, or **View → WellSheet**.
- It can also be viewed in the patient's chart from the **inpatient workflow** or the bottom of the **Table of Contents**.
- A QR code in the original links to a short demo.

**Helpful features:**
- User-driven Smart Notifications on a mobile device
- Printed handoff list
- Enhanced multidisciplinary care-team collaboration
- Team care coordination and discharge planning

## Handoff Notes
Select the **Handoff Type** from the dropdown. Available types: **Case Management, Emergency Department, Hospitalist, Nursing, Surgery, Trauma** Handoff Notes.

> **IMPORTANT:** You must still complete Provider notes from the patient's chart in Cerner — the handoff note does not replace them.

Controls on the handoff screen include List Filtering, a **List / Full** toggle, **Toggle Handoff Notes**, **Edit Handoff**, and List Printing. Within a patient:
- **Handoff** tab — the relevant handoff notes (e.g., Hospitalist Notes) and Hospitalist Tasks.
- **My Notes** tab — Note to Self, Draft A&P, and a **Draft Hospital Course**.

> **NOTE on the Draft Hospital Course:** this is **AI-generated**. WellSheet displays the disclaimer that the summary was generated using AI and the patient's chart must be reviewed by a medical professional for accuracy. Use **Generate** to create it and **Copy hospital course** to copy it.

## Select a patient — review information
Select the **chevron** to review labs, diagnostics, notes, vital signs, and other details. Use the left **Navigate** pane (Recent, Meds, Results, Docs, Views). From here you can also reach:
- **Mobile Notifications** (prefilled with chart data; also on mobile with real-time updates)
- **Printable Handoff**
- **Risk Calculators** (prefilled with chart data; copy results to your notes)
- **Pinned Views** and condition-specific **Select Views** (e.g., AF, DM, Hosp, Card, CHF, Depression, HTN, Sepsis, and more), each editable via **Edit View**.

## Discharge planning
Open discharge planning from the patient card.
- **Medically Cleared:** Not Cleared / Cleared
- **Discharge Date:** No status / Today / Tomorrow / Other date (auto-updates at midnight)
- **Discharge Disposition** (e.g., Home Self Care)
- **Clinical Barriers** and **Discharge Barriers** (e.g., Biopsy Result, Transportation)
- **Status Flags:** Pregnancy, Foley Catheter, CVC (Central Line), PW (Pacer Wires), CTs (Chest Tubes), POD, I-PASS

**Discharge View** is reached via the **Discharges** tab in the top navigation. The census/discharge list can be sorted by clicking any column header (Name/FIN, Discharge Date, Location, Status, LOS/GM, AMPAC, Medically Cleared, Disposition, Payor, Clinical Barriers, Discharge Barriers).

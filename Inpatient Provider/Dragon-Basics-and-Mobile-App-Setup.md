# Dragon Medical One — Basics & Mobile App Setup

**Audience:** General use
**Date:** Apr 25, 2022

## Overview
Getting started with Dragon Medical One (DMO). If you need Dragon access, contact your manager or the medical staff office (a provisioning request/license is required).

## Microphones
At first login, you choose which available microphone to use. Any mic works, but **PowerMic Mobile** is recommended for best accuracy. Options include the mobile mic on a personal smartphone and the USB corded PowerMic II/III.

> **TROUBLESHOOTING:** If your microphone isn't listed, check your computer's microphone input settings.

## Use Dragon with Cerner
You must launch via the **Dragon-integrated Cerner icons**, which open both Cerner and Dragon at once.

1. Click a **Dragon PowerChart, FirstNet, or SurgiNet** icon.
   - **NOTE:** Dragon requires a provisioning request for a license. No Dragon Cerner icons? Request access from your manager or the medical staff office.
   - From the Cerner apps page (`http://cernerapps.sjmc.org/Citrix/ProdWeb/`), open the **Dragon** folder to find these applications.
2. In the Dragon Medical One window, verify your username.
3. Under **Microphone**, choose your mic:
   - **PowerMicII-NS** = the USB handheld microphone.
   - **Nuance PowerMic Mobile** = the phone app.
4. Log into Cerner.

## Use Dragon with other applications
> **INSTALL REQUIRED:** To dictate into non-Cerner apps (email, Google Docs, etc.), request the **Stand-Alone Dragon Extension**. Once installed, you can dictate into virtually any application.

1. Click the desktop **Dragon Medical One** icon and log in.
2. Place your cursor where you want text.
3. Click the microphone icon and begin speaking.
4. If your text editor isn't fully supported, dictation goes to the dictation box; when finished, say **"transfer text"** to move it into your editor.

## PowerMic Mobile app
When no physical Dragon mic is present, the mobile app connects to your computer's "Cerner Dragon PowerChart" session for dictation. Download the app, then scan the QR code to add the St. John profile.

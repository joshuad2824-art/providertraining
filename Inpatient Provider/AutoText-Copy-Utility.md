# AutoText Copy Utility

**Audience:** Providers, Scribes
**System:** Cerner
**Published:** 4/21/2020

## What it does
The AutoText Copy Utility lets any provider or scribe manage AutoText. With it you can:
- Search another user's personal or shared AutoText and copy one, several, or all of their phrases.
- View your own library to remove one or all AutoText phrases.
- Quickly launch the **Manage Auto Text** dialog to update your library.

## How to copy another provider's AutoText
> In the original job aid, each step is paired with a screenshot (not included in this text version).

1. From the Organizer, select the **Auto Text Copy Utility** (also accessible from the **View** menu).
2. In the **search user** field on the left, type the name of the provider whose AutoText you want to copy.
3. Click the AutoText, then click **Copy**.
   - If the provider has a long list, use the **search auto text** field to find a specific phrase.
   - To copy two: hold **Shift** while clicking multiple phrases.
   - To copy all: check the **select all** box.
4. In the **Copy Auto Text** window, change the abbreviation and description if desired.
5. Click **Log out**. A log out is required between copying AutoTexts.

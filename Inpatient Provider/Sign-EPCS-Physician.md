# Sign EPCS — Electronically Prescribing a Controlled Medication

**Audience:** All Physicians
**System:** Cerner PowerChart
**Cerner update effective:** 12.19.2019

## Background
On January 1, 2020, Oklahoma House Bill 2931 took effect, requiring that **all controlled substances (Schedule II–V) be electronically prescribed**. The steps below cover prescribing controlled substances from Cerner.

## Steps to ePrescribe
> In the original job aid, each step is paired with a screenshot (not included in this text version).

1. Add order.
2. Switch category to "Discharge Meds as Rx."
3. Search for the order.
4. Verify the pharmacy with the patient.
   - The preferred pharmacy should default into the **Send To** field.
   - If no pharmacy is selected, click **Find Pharmacy**.
   - To change the pharmacy, use the change-pharmacy option.
5. Complete the **Order Details** and sign.
6. The **Electronic Prescription Preview** window displays. Place a checkmark next to each medication and sign.
7. Enter your network password in the **Confirm your identity** window.
8. Approve the push notification sent to your device.
   - You can also approve on an Apple Watch.
   - If the push notification has expired or that feature is off, you'll get the **Confirm your identity** window asking for an Imprivata token code.

## APRNs & PAs — Schedule II substances
In Oklahoma, Advanced Practice Providers (APPs) **cannot** send prescriptions for Schedule II substances.
- They can electronically prescribe Schedule III–V, which route directly to the pharmacy.
- For Schedule II, they can start the prescription, right-click and change it to a **Proposal**, then enter a physician's name. This routes the prescription to that provider's Message Center. The Rx will **not** route to the pharmacy until the physician signs it.

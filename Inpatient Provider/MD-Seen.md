# MD Seen

**Audience:** Emergency Room Providers
**System:** Cerner FirstNet
**Go-live:** 3/18/2026

## Overview
How to complete **MD Seen**. The time documented here establishes the **door-to-doc time** (the interval from patient arrival to first provider evaluation — a key ED throughput and quality metric).

## Steps
1. In the **Provider Notification** column for the patient you're starting on, select the provider notification.
   > The icon indicates that MD Seen still needs to be completed.
2. Click the blue **Document**.
3. Document the date and time, then select the **teal check mark** to sign the MD Seen.
   - **TIP:** Typing **T** in the date field pulls in today's date; typing **N** in the time field enters the current time.

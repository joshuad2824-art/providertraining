About PowerChart

One record, one chart

PowerChart is the electronic health record you will use for the patients you care for. Your care team shares one record, so you don’t have to repeat yourself.

In PowerChart you will review results, place orders and PowerPlans, reconcile medications, document your assessment, and create and sign notes.

PowerChart

The main chart. Inpatient, ambulatory and specialty views all live here.

FirstNet

The emergency department view, built around the ED tracking board, called LaunchPoint.

Your Cerner position

Perioperative and specialty content is built into PowerChart and released by Cerner position. What you see is set by the position you were given, not by a separate application.

Whichever application you open, you are looking at the same patient record — only the view and the starting screen change. Ascension St. John runs the 2018.01 build, so Cerner screenshots and tips you find online may not match your screen.
About PowerChart

Who else is in the chart

You are one of many people documenting on the same patient. Knowing who does what saves you duplicate work.

Providers

Physicians, mid‑levels and residents. Orders, workflow documentation, notes, discharge.

Nursing

Assessments, medication administration, patient education, and orders taken by phone or verbally that come to you to sign.

Pharmacy

Reviews and verifies medication orders. Pharmacy technicians often complete the medication history before you review it.

Ancillary and therapy

Lab, imaging, respiratory, rehab and case management document in the same chart and results flow back to you.
Getting in

Access PowerChart

How you open PowerChart depends on where you are.

On an Ascension workstation

Open PowerChart from the desktop, or from the Citrix Portal icon for MVD applications. On site, join the AH-AIA network — your Wi‑Fi username is your domain\username.

From home

Install Citrix Workspace, then sign in at cloud.ascension.org with your domain\username. The old mvd.sjmc.org address is being retired. To view radiology images off site, open the OKTUL Published Desktop under Desktops rather than launching the viewer from Cerner.

On your phone

Provider phone applications include PowerChart Touch, Message Center, Dragon Medical One and PerfectServe. Obstetrics also uses FetaLink+.

Set your password, and set up EPCS with Imprivata if you prescribe controlled substances. Clinical Informatics can help: 918-744-3088, Monday to Friday.

Getting in

Select your role

Cerner asks which role you are working in. Your answer sets the view you land on.

Choose the provider role that matches the work you are about to do. Most providers land in the Message Center; in the emergency department you land on the ED tracking board, and in a perioperative area you land in case selection.

Role selection window
If you select the wrong role, your default view and available tools will not match your work. Close PowerChart and reopen it to choose again.
The patient chart

The PowerChart window

Five things to find before you do anything else.

1
Menu bar and toolbar

Task, Edit, View, Patient, Chart, Links, Notifications, Inbox and Help across the very top, with the toolbar underneath.

2
Message Center

Where you land at login. Inbox, Proxies and Pools tabs, with the Inbox Summary listing the documents, orders, results and refill requests waiting on you.

3
Patient Lists

Opens your patient lists — your patients, and the patients on your unit. This is the primary way you reach a chart.

4
Role tools

MPTL, Physician Worklist, PC TOUCH, Depart and Communicate sit on the toolbar. What appears depends on your role.

5
Search

Top right. Use it for a patient who is not on one of your lists.

The patient chart

The patient chart tab

Every chart you open becomes its own tab.

The open patient’s tab
List, Recent and the Name search box
1

The tab shows the patient name. Check it before you enter anything. With several charts open it is easy to type into the wrong one.

2

Close the tab, not the application, when you finish with a patient. Closing charts you are done with keeps the window manageable.

3

List, Recent and the Name box sit at the top right. Recent is the fastest way back to a chart you just had open; use Name to search for a patient who is not on a list.
The patient chart

The demographics bar

The band across the top of every chart. Read it every time you open a patient.

One continuous strip, shown in two halves so it stays readable. Left half above, right half below.
1
Name, Age, DOB, Sex

Identity, on the left. Confirm it against the patient in front of you before you document.

2
MRN and Fin#

MRN identifies the person and never changes. Fin# identifies this encounter, so it changes with every admission.

3
Allergies

Far right, above the encounter type and admission date and time. Check it before ordering — “no known allergies” and a blank field are not the same thing.

4
Code Status, Loc, Isolation

Code Status and Portal on the second row. Loc gives unit, room and bed. Isolation shows any precautions in place.

5
PCP and Attending

Who else is on this patient. These relationships drive whose patient list the patient appears on.
The patient chart

The two left-hand menus

Two menus sit on the left. The chart Menu collapses to a vertical tab at the far edge; inside Provider View, a second list holds the workflow components.

Provider View component list
Menu (the far-left tab)

The chart table of contents. Click the vertical Menu tab to expand it, then pin it open with the pin icon. Results Review, Orders, Medication List, Histories and Documentation live here.

Workflow tabs

Admit Inpt WF, ClinPic, Manage Inpt WF and any specialty view run across the top of Provider View.

Component list

Labs, New Order Entry, Chief Complaint, Home Medications, Medications, History of Present Illness, Review of Systems. Click one to jump to that section of the page.

Reorder it to suit you

You can drag components into the order you prefer and it sticks to your account. Training domain settings reset overnight.

Documentation

All notes on the chart, reached from Menu. In the live record this appears as Provider Note.
Documenting

Documentation and notes

Where notes live, and what the buttons at the bottom of a note actually do.

Sign / Submit

Completes the note and makes it part of the record.

Save

Holds the note for you to finish later. Other providers cannot see a saved note.

Revise or Addend

Find the note under Documentation, select it, and choose Modify to revise or add to it.

In Error

For a note created on the wrong patient or in the wrong place. Add a comment explaining why.

Forward

The Action Pane defaults to Sign. Use Other Forwarding Options to send the note to another provider to Review or Sign, then find them with the binoculars search.

Your notes route to an attending to sign. Forward from the Action Pane and choose Sign, then search the attending by last name, first name.

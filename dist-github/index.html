Something wrong or missing?

Every topic here is transcribed from a published job aid. If a screen has changed or a step is wrong, that is worth knowing — raise it with the OKTUL provider training team so the source aid and this site stay in step.

The screenshots use test patients. Every Cerner screen shown here comes from the training domain, and the PerfectServe login screens have the tester's email and phone number blurred at source. Nothing on this site is real patient information.
